## Reflection

The earliest visual and actin signals were right: describe_phenotype e4 flagged Actin disruptors, and high-dose actin texture exploded with act_glcm_contrast z=+67.5 at 30 uM (PMID 6893016) and act_mean dose rho=+1.00 alongside cell rounding and cell_area rho=-1.00. I also correctly recorded multinucleation with frac_multinucleate_proxy z=+8.4 at 1 uM, which is consistent with actin-inhibition-induced polyploidization (PMID 9568120).

I was misled by parallel tubulin hyperintensity into a Microtubule stabilizer call: tub_mean z=+31.3 at 3 uM and z=+44.6 at 30 uM with rho=+1.00. As the review noted, tub and act both rose with severe rounding, suggesting fluorophore concentration rather than stabilizer-specific bundles, and atlas e5 was ambiguous with stabilizers only 3/10 and top neighbour Cholesterol-lowering. Acquiring only replicate 1 per dose left no way to test stability.

I then overcorrected to Protein synthesis on a single-well atlas e17 with 8/10 neighbours at 3 uM, ignoring that e19 at 30 uM reverted to stabilizers 3/10 and e20 at 1 uM gave Epithelial 5/10. That dose-varying, n=1 atlas flip plus the failure to weigh direct actin filament shortening activity (PMID 8204105) caused the final miss of true Actin disruptors.

What was right:
- e4 microscopist call of Actin disruptors captured the true class early, consistent with massive actin texture change e2 act_glcm_contrast z=+67.5 and e14 z=+36.0
- Tracked dose-dependent actin rise e9 rho=+1.00 and e22 act_glcm_contrast rho=+1.00 with cell contraction e11 rho=-1.00 and count loss e21 rho=-0.90, correctly noting rounding/concentration artifact risk
- Recorded multinucleate proxy e3 z=+8.4 and dose trend e10 rho=+0.80, a real cytochalasin B polyploidization phenotype

What was wrong or missed:
- Interpreted tub_mean escalation e6 z=+31.3 and e2 z=+44.6 with e8 rho=+1.00 as stabilizer-specific while ignoring identical act_mean escalation, leading to first wrong conclusion Microtubule stabilizers p=0.58
- Overweighted ambiguous atlas e5 (stabilizers 3/10) despite top neighbour being Cholesterol-lowering and e4 favoring Actin disruptors, then flipped to Protein synthesis p=0.45 on single-well e17 8/10 without replicates
- Used only replicate 1 per dose with per-well atlas comparisons e17-e20 that varied by dose, and never acquired replicate wells or actin-specific counterstain to resolve stabilizer vs actin vs protein synthesis

Proposed next experiment: Repeat low-dose titration with phalloidin F-actin vs acetylated-tubulin co-stain plus washout and a puromycylation nascent-protein control to test for actin filament loss/fragmentation without microtubule bundling or translation shutdown.
