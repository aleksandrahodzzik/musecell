## Reflection

The high-dose acquisition at 3 uM (137 cells, tub_glcm_contrast z=+121.7, tub_std z=+69.7) was decisive because bright textured tubulin is directionally opposite to the diffuse loss predicted for destabilizers. The mid-dose at 0.1 uM (tub_std z=+33.1, tub_mean z=+18.3) and microscopist report of bundling plus atlas match of 5/10 Microtubule stabilizers converged early, which fits unblinded epothilone B as a microtubule stabilizer causing mitotic arrest (PMID 14522931).

Choosing the low-intermediate 0.01 uM well (433 cells, tub_glcm_contrast z=+5.5) and testing dose_response for tub_glcm_contrast and tub_mean (both Spearman rho=+1.00) was the right acquisition choice over a replicate, because monotonic increase directly tested stabilizer vs destabilizer and raised Microtubule stabilizers from 0.88 to 0.93.

What was weak was leaving the large nuc_solidity drop (z=-39.8 at high dose) and residual Protein degradation hypothesis (0.03-0.04) untested, and wasting a step on a literature search with 0 hits. Stabilization can involve Tau-dependent mechanisms and tubulin-isotype sensitivity (PMID 41673684, PMID 21738778), so bundling and intensity alone do not prove binding site.

What was right:
- Prioritized high-dose 3 uM well first, revealing extreme tubulin bundling texture (tub_glcm_contrast z=+121.7, tub_std z=+69.7) opposite to destabilizer prediction
- Used mid-dose 0.1 uM and low-dose 0.01 uM to show early onset and monotonic dose increase in tub_glcm_contrast and tub_mean (rho=+1.00)
- Combined visual bundling from describe_phenotype with compare_to_reference_atlas 5/10 stabilizer neighbors to separate stabilizers from destabilizers and Aurora/Eg5 inhibitors

What was wrong or missed:
- Did not follow up the very low nuc_solidity z=-39.8 and lower cell count (137 vs 433 cells) with mitotic or nuclear-shape measurement to explain mitotic arrest morphology
- Left Protein degradation at 0.03-0.04 and Cholesterol-lowering atlas hit unaddressed without an explicit discriminating test
- Ran a second literature search on paclitaxel bundling/acetylated tubulin that returned 0 hits instead of banking the 5 epothilone B papers already found

Proposed next experiment: Fixed dose-titration of epothilone B vs paclitaxel control with anti-alpha-tubulin plus anti-acetylated-tubulin immunofluorescence, EB1 comets for dynamics, and phospho-H3 mitotic index to confirm stabilized bundled microtubules, suppressed dynamics, and mitotic arrest.
