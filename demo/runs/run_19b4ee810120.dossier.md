## Reflection

Low-dose sampling got the call right. Wells w_80f45bbd63, w_7d742a2ea2 and replicate w_3559cc7c5c showed extreme frac_large_nucleus z=+70 to +138 with nuc_area z=+38 to +46 and low mitotic signal, plus microscopist binucleate/enlarged nuclei and 9/10 Aurora neighbors on compare_to_reference_atlas (PMID 19369091). Replicating the 0.1 uM phenotype was the decisive acquisition choice, lifting Aurora to 0.93 and correctly ruling out Eg5 and microtubule destabilizers.

The high-dose extension was misleading but did not flip the call. Well w_bb49de52e8 at 30 uM added tub_glcm_contrast z=+28.9 and act_glcm_contrast z=+22.6, a second describe_phenotype call of Microtubule stabilizers, and an isolated-well atlas of 8/10 Microtubule stabilizers, while nuc_area dose_response rho=-0.97 showed the large-nucleus signal falling at the top dose. I treated this as biphasic secondary toxicity rather than primary MoA, but it correctly forced confidence down to 0.85.

Lesson is dose-range weighting: primary MoA signal was low-dose polyploidy with intact cytoskeleton, while cytoskeletal texture only emerged at 30 uM. Future runs should pre-plan low-dose replication before spending on the top dose, and pair the top dose with a direct tubulin assay rather than atlas alone.

What was right:
- Prioritized 0.1-1 uM wells where frac_large_nucleus z=+98.8 to +138.6 and nuc_area z=+38 to +46 defined polyploidy signal (e2, e3, e12).
- Used describe_phenotype enlarged/binucleate calls with intact MT/actin to favor Aurora over Eg5/MT agents (e5).
- Used 9/10 Aurora reference-atlas matches to anchor hypothesis at 0.88 then 0.93 (e6, e16).
- Replicated 0.1 uM phenotype in rep 2 well w_3559cc7c5c to exclude well artifact (e12).
- Used low frac_mitotic_like dose trend rho=+0.50 p=0.67 to argue against Eg5 mitotic arrest (e8).

- lookup_compound and literature searches added no discriminative value: AZ258 had no curated ChEMBL mechanisms (e22) and searches returned unrelated or zero hits for the polyploidy phenotype (e23).

Proposed next experiment: Aurora B target-engagement titration by phospho-histone H3 Ser10 immunofluorescence plus time-lapse mitosis to confirm cytokinesis failure and polyploidy, paired with in vitro Aurora A/B kinase assay and tubulin-polymerization counter-assay across 0.1 to 30 uM to test whether the 30 uM microtubule-bundling signal is direct stabilization or secondary toxicity.
